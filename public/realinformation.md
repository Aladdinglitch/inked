## **Booking & Client Intake**

### **Preferred Booking Methods**

* Online Contact Form
* Phone Call
* Direct Message (Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp)

### **Booking Information**

Thank you for choosing **INKED ATTRACTION Tattoo & Piercing Studio**. To ensure a smooth booking experience, please complete the booking form with accurate details about your tattoo or piercing request.

### **For Tattoo Appointments**

Please include:

* Your tattoo idea or concept
* Preferred placement
* Approximate size
* Black & Grey or Colour
* Reference images or inspiration (if available)

### **For Piercing Appointments**

Please include:

* Desired piercing
* Jewellery preference (if applicable)
* Any questions or concerns

### **Booking Policy**

* A **50% non-refundable deposit** is required to secure all appointments.
* Deposits are deducted from the final service cost.
* Appointments are confirmed only after the deposit has been received.

### **Rescheduling & Cancellations**

* Please provide at least **48 hours' notice** to reschedule.
* Deposits may be transferred to a new appointment when sufficient notice is given.
* Missed appointments or late cancellations may result in the forfeiture of the deposit.

### **Before Your Appointment**

* Eat a proper meal and stay hydrated.
* Wear comfortable clothing that provides easy access to the tattoo or piercing area.
* Arrive on time for your appointment.

### **Aftercare**

Detailed aftercare instructions will be provided after your tattoo or piercing. Following these guidelines is essential to ensure proper healing and achieve the best possible results.
